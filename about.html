Trade Heritage Since 1975

50+ Years of Practical Fastener Expertise

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation (GLNBC) was founded in 1975 in Mumbai. For over five decades, we have been a steadfast partner to civil infrastructure contractors, plant engineers, fabrication workshops, and heavy engineering firms across India.

Built on Practical Trade Competence

In industrial fasteners and hardware, reliability is measured not by claims, but by precision, grade integrity, and dependable supply. Whether supplying high-pressure ASTM A193 B7 stud bolts for a petrochemical shutdown, heavy foundation anchor bolts for a structural tower, or custom-bent U-bolts for a plumbing contractor, GLNBC delivers components that match exact engineering criteria.

Our Core Principles

Standard & Custom Versatility

We maintain off-the-shelf inventories of standard IS, DIN, and ISO fasteners, while possessing the tooling and supplier network to deliver custom lengths, thread pitches, and specialized bends to drawing.

Direct Human Sales

No automated telephone mazes or rigid checkout restrictions. You speak directly with experienced personnel who understand thread sizes, proof loads, and material compatibility.

3 Mumbai Trade Counters

Our physical presence across Kumbharwada, Nagdevi Street, and Bibijan Street provides contractors and purchasing teams with immediate counter pickup and sample checking.

Transparent Commercials

Competitive trade pricing subject to current raw material rates, with volume and cash settlement discounts openly discussed.

Product Range at a Glance

Our comprehensive product inventory includes:

  • U-Bolts & Clamps: Round bend, square bend, two-bolt pipe clamps, EPDM rubber-lined acoustic clamps, hi-tech clamps, double-wire hose clamps, and saddle straps.
  • Structural Fasteners & Bolts: Hex bolts (Grade 4.6, 8.8, 10.9, 12.9), Allen cap screws, CSK bolts, Eye bolts, T-bolts, and civil Foundation bolts.
  • Studs & Heavy Nuts: ASTM A193 B7 continuous threaded rods, double-end studs, ASTM A194 2H Heavy Hex nuts, Long coupling nuts, Nylock nuts, and Wing nuts.
  • Anchors & Fixing Systems: Expansion wedge anchors, hammer-drive Pin-type anchors, bullet fasteners, and Rawl bolt shield fixings.
  • Washers & Gaskets: Bitumen (Dambar) washers, Spring washers, Plain washers, serrated Rip-Lock washers, and domed Umbrella washers.
  • Specialty Screws & Brackets: Self-drilling Tek screws, Button head screws, C-channel brackets (C Patti), and Z-brackets.
