Trade Heritage Since 1975

50+ Years of Practical Fastener Expertise

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation (GLNBC) was founded in 1975 in Mumbai. For over five decades, we have been a steadfast partner to civil infrastructure contractors, plant engineers, fabrication workshops, and heavy engineering firms across India.

Built on Practical Trade Competence

In industrial fasteners and hardware, reliability is measured not by claims, but by precision, grade integrity, and dependable supply. Whether supplying high-pressure ASTM A193 B7 stud bolts for a petrochemical shutdown, heavy foundation anchor bolts for a structural tower, or custom-bent U-bolts for a plumbing contractor, GLNBC delivers components that match exact engineering criteria.

Product Range at a Glance

Our comprehensive 41-product inventory includes:

  • U-Bolts & Clamps: Round bend, square bend, two-bolt pipe clamps, EPDM rubber-lined acoustic clamps, hi-tech clamps, and saddle straps.
  • Structural Fasteners & Bolts: Hex bolts (Grade 4.6, 8.8, 10.9, 12.9), Allen cap screws, CSK bolts, Eye bolts, and civil Foundation bolts.
  • Studs & Heavy Nuts: ASTM A193 B7 continuous threaded rods, double-end studs, ASTM A194 2H Heavy Hex nuts, Long coupling nuts, and Lock nuts.
  • Anchors & Fixing Systems: Expansion wedge anchors, hammer-drive Pin-type anchors, and Rawl bolt shield fixings.
  • Washers & Gaskets: Bitumen (Dambar) washers, PVC rubber sealing washers, serrated Rip-Lock washers, and domed Umbrella washers.
  • Specialty Screws & Brackets: Self-drilling Tek screws, Button head screws, C-channel brackets (C Patti), and Z-brackets.
