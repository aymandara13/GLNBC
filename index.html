Flagship Specialist Line

Mumbai's Specialist in Industrial U-Bolts & Pipe Clamping Solutions

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation is recognized across industrial markets for manufacturing and supplying heavy-duty U-Bolts and industrial pipe support clamps. We cater to piping contractors, infrastructure projects, and automotive chassis fabricators with exact dimensions and specialized anti-corrosion coatings.

  • Geometries: Round bend, square bend, semi-round, and custom saddle profiles for 1/2" to 24"+ NB pipes.
  • Materials: Mild Steel (IS 2062), Stainless Steel (SS 304, 316), High-Tensile Alloy (EN8, EN19, B7).
  • Threading: Standard thread lengths, extra-long threads, full threads, cut & rolled threading.
  • Assemblies: Supplied bare or complete with matching hex nuts, 2H heavy nuts, spring washers, and backing plates.
