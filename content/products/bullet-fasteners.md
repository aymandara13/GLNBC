# Bullet Fasteners / TS Anchors

GLNBC stocks industrial bullet fasteners (drop-in expansion anchors / TS anchors). Features an internally threaded tubular body containing an internal expansion cone. Once driven in, the anchor expands to grip solid concrete, providing a permanent flush internal thread for studs and bolts.
