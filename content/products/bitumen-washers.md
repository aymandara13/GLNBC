# Bitumen & Rubber Washers

GLNBC supplies heavy-duty Bitumen and Rubber Washers (locally known as Dambar Washers) manufactured for watertight sealing of industrial roofing sheets, PEB claddings, and purlin tie-downs. Formed from flexible elastomeric rubber or bitumen-impregnated fiber felt.
