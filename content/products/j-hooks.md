# J-Hooks

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation manufactures standard and PVC-coated threaded J-Hooks designed for securing corrugated metal and fiber cement roofing sheets to structural steel purlins. Supplied in standard and custom lengths with matching hex nuts, umbrella washers, and bitumen waterproofing pads.
