# Rubber Pipe Clamps

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation stocks premium EPDM rubber-lined pipe clamps engineered to DIN 4109 acoustic standards. The vulcanized elastomeric rubber profile dampens vibration noise from pumps, water hammer, and fluid velocity, while providing electrical isolation.
