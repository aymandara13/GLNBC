# Channel Trays & Cable Trays

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation supplies heavy-gauge pre-galvanized and hot-dip galvanized perforated cable trays, channel trays, and cable trunking systems. Provides continuous structural routing, heat dissipation, and mechanical protection for power and control cables.
