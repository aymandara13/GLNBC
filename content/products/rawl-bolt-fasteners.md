# Rawl Bolt Fasteners

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation supplies heavy-duty pressed-steel shield expansion anchors (Rawl Bolts). Designed for heavy masonry fixings, the segment shields expand outward over 360 degrees as the internal cone is pulled inward by the bolt.
