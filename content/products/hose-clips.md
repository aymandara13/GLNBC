# Hose Clips

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation supplies high-torque worm-drive and wire hose clips engineered for sealing flexible rubber, PVC, and silicone hoses over rigid spigots. Constructed with smooth-edged bands and captive worm screws to prevent hose pinching.
