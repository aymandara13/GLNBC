# U-Bolts

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation is Mumbai's established specialist in precision-formed industrial U-Bolts since 1975. We manufacture and supply heavy-duty round bend, square bend, and custom profile U-bolts engineered for rigid pipe support, conduit mounting, process plant headers, and commercial vehicle chassis clamping. Supplied in Mild Steel, Stainless Steel (SS 304, SS 316), and High-Tensile Alloy grades with standard metric/imperial threads or custom extended threading to drawing.
