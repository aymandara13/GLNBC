# PVC Caps

Flexible, high-durability molded PVC and plastic end caps designed to cover exposed ends of strut channels (41x41mm, 41x21mm), pipe ends, scaffolding tubes, and exposed bolt threads. Protects personnel from sharp burrs and prevents ingress of dust.
