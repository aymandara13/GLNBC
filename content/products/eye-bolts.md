# Eye Bolts

High-integrity forged lifting eye bolts manufactured to DIN 580 and BS specifications. Available in standard collar type, fully threaded machine type, and extended shank variants for direct axial and angled lifting of heavy machinery, electrical transformers, motors, and rigging assemblies.
