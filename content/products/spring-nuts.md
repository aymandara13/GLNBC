# Strut Channel Spring Nuts (Zeb Nuts)

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation supplies precision-grooved Strut Channel Spring Nuts (locally known as Zeb Nuts). Features a rectangular nut body with grooved serrated edges and an attached helical spring that holds the nut firmly in position inside 41x41mm and 41x21mm strut channels.
