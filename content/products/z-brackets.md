# Z-Brackets / Z-Patti

Formed metal Z-brackets (Z-Patti) designed to provide rigid offset mounting for solar photovoltaic panels, exterior facade cladding, and suspended ceiling profiles. Kept strictly distinct from generic wall brackets, the stepped Z-profile allows unobstructed airflow and wiring clearance.
