# Hi-Tech Clamps

Engineered for high-stress piping support, Hi-Tech Clamps incorporate stiffening ribs and heavier gauge steel sections to achieve maximum load capacities with minimal deflection. Ideal for high-pressure industrial lines, fire sprinkler mains, and mechanical headers.
