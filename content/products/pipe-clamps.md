# Pipe Clamps / Patti Clamps / Saddle Clamps

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation manufactures heavy-gauge industrial Pipe Clamps, Patti Clamps, and Saddle Clamps. Available in Single-bolt and Double-bolt configurations, these clamps provide flush, rigid mounting and suspension of process pipes, electrical conduits, and water lines to walls, ceilings, and strut channels.
