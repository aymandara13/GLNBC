# Anchor Fasteners (Wedge)

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation stocks high-performance wedge anchor fasteners (through bolts) designed for heavy structural anchoring in solid concrete, granite, and dense masonry. Features a precision-engineered expansion clip that bites securely into concrete walls as the nut is torqued.
