# Foundation Bolts

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation fabricates high-capacity structural foundation bolts engineered for embedding into concrete foundations, column base plates, transmission towers, and heavy industrial machinery beds. Fabricated in mild steel (IS 2062), high-tensile alloy steel (EN8, Grade 8.8), and stainless steel.
