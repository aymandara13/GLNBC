# Saddle Clamps

Pressed metal saddle clamps engineered for neat, low-profile securing of electrical conduits, plumbing pipes, and copper tubing directly to walls, ceilings, and structural steel beams. Available in two-hole full saddle and one-hole half saddle variants.
