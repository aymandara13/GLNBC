# Spring Washers

GLNBC supplies industrial split helical spring lock washers manufactured to DIN 127 and IS 3063. The split spring ends bite into fastener bearing surfaces under torque, exerting continuous spring preload to resist loosening under mechanical vibration and thermal cycles.
