# Umbrella Washers

Good Luck Nut Bolt Corporation supplies pressed galvanized metal umbrella washers (curved dome roofing washers). Shaped to cradle the crest of corrugated roofing sheets, these washers compress bitumen or EPDM rubber pads firmly against the sheet surface.
