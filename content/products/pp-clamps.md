# PP (Polypropylene) Clamps

Standard and heavy series Polypropylene (PP) clamp bodies manufactured to DIN 3015 standards. Provides exceptional vibration damping, noise reduction, and corrosion isolation for hydraulic lines, fuel pipes, and instrumentation tubing.
