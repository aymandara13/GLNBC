# Hex Bolts & High-Tensile Bolts

GLNBC supplies industrial hexagon head bolts engineered to international dimensional and mechanical standards. Available across low-carbon commercial grades, high-tensile alloy steels (8.8, 10.9, 12.9, EN8, EN19), and austenitic stainless steels (SS 304, SS 316) for machinery, structural steelwork, and fabrication.
